Who we are

We are a concert ticket platform that are made for fans. We were founded in 2020 and serves music lovers across the world. Instead of searching for tickets on multiple websites, overwhelming with waiting rooms, hidden fees and bots which creates anxiety, we want to create memorable experience among fans and artists. So We have only one mission which is to let music fans buying concert tickets easily and securely.

Our long-term vision is to become the standard for concert ticketing, replacing opaque resale markets with transparent, secure, and direct access to the artists you love. Each bot we block, each hidden fee we remove, is a step toward our real goal: the freedom to just enjoy the show.
